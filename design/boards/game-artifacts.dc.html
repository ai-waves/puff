Hollowdrift

A side-scrolling physics flyer for phones. You guide Puff, a seed with a sprout on its head, through dark overgrown worlds. Hold to puff upward, collect seeds, and get as many clones home as you can.

FormatLandscape iPhone, 844 × 390 pt
InputHold anywhere to puff. Release to drift.

Play

Each world has 20 levels laid out on a scrolling vine path. The three dots under a level are its seeds. Puff waits at the next level, and tapping a level opens a small card with its goal before play.
A4Gameplay HUD · 1-4
×3

The HUD has three parts: pause, level progress (the tick marks the checkpoint), and seeds plus a live clone count. Everything sits in the top 70pt so thumbs on the lower screen never cover it. Hazards are pure black. Things that help you glow.

The world stays visible behind a dark overlay so pausing doesn't break the mood. Resume is the only filled button. Settings are three toggles here, with no separate settings screen mid-level.

The level ends at a glowing burrow and the sky brightens. Clones saved is the main result. Seeds fill in one at a time with a soft chime, and the next level is one tap away.
A7Fail · last clone lost
Shredded. The thornwheel got your last clone.
Retry from checkpoint
Restart

Failing should be quick and a bit funny, never a punishment. The sky drains of warmth, the cause of death gets a one-word title, and the main button returns you to the last checkpoint in under a second. No lives, no timers.

Puff · character sheet

A dandelion seed that wandered too far from home
PLACEHOLDER BUILD · FINAL ART BY ILLUSTRATOR
SilhouetteRound body with a fuzzy rim of 8 tufts. It should read clearly at 24pt against black.
SignatureA sprout on its head is the only green in the game. It droops when Puff is hurt and perks up in light.
PersonalityCurious and easily startled. One big eye looks toward the next hazard, which doubles as a gameplay tell.
