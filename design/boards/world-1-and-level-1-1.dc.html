Physics stays the same across all 40 levels, so Puff always feels the same to control. Only the world gets tighter and faster. Bigger jumps in difficulty come from each chapter's new mechanic, not from the numbers.

B

World 1 · Mossdeep

Levels 1–10 · The meadow floor where Puff's dandelion fell
L0 SKY · 0.0× PARALLAX L1 FAR · 0.2× L2 MID · 0.5× L3 PLAY · 1.0× · COLLIDES L4 FORE · 1.4× · NEVER ON PATH
Story beat

A gust tore Puff from its dandelion. Mossdeep is the warm, low meadow where it lands. It's safe and soft, and slowly getting darker. The sun sets across the world: level 1 is golden hour and level 10 is the last light.

Sky per level · 1 → 10
World rules
Only L3 collides. It is always the darkest layer, with no exceptions. Ambient life stays dull. Moths and crickets are drawn in the mid-layer color. Only things that help you glow. Foreground never covers the flight path or anything you can pick up. Audio: low wind and crickets. A music-box melody adds one voice for each living clone.
Terrain vocabulary

D1Beat 01 · Wake, first-run hint
Hold

Puff sleeps with its eye closed. The level waits until the first hold, which wakes Puff and starts the scroll. The hint uses a single word and fades on release. It only comes back if the player hasn't held for 4 seconds.

D2Beat 04 · Hollow log
Tap to stay level

The log shades the HUD, so its ink turns pale while Puff is inside. The second and last hint is printed on the log floor as part of the world, not as an overlay. The burrow's glow is already visible at the far end.
